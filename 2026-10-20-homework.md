## Part 1: LED and Resistor Circuit Analysis

### Question:
In a circuit with one LED, one resistor, a 5V source, and ground, does it matter whether the resistor is connected before or after the LED? What causes the LED to burn out?

### Explanation:
1. Resistor Placement:
   - It DOES NOT matter whether the resistor is placed before the LED (5V -> Resistor -> LED -> Ground) or after the LED (5V -> LED -> Resistor -> Ground).
   - Reason: In a single-loop series circuit, according to Kirchhoff's Current Law, the electric current (I) is identical through all components. The resistor limits the overall current of the circuit regardless of its position relative to the LED.

2. Mechanism of Burnout (Heat and Power):
   - The LED burns out due to thermal destruction of its p-n junction caused by excessive current.
   - Without a current-limiting resistor, the current (I) increases rapidly.
   - Power dissipated as heat is given by: Power = Voltage * Current (P = V * I) or Power = Current^2 * Resistance (P = I^2 * R).
   - When the power exceeds the maximum rating of the LED, the heat destroys the semiconductor crystal structure.

## Part 2: Building Basic Logic Gates using 74HC00 (Quad 2-Input NAND Gates)

The 74HC00 IC consists of four independent 2-input NAND gates: Output = NOT (A AND B).
Below is the implementation of NOT, AND, OR, and XOR gates using only NAND logic.

1. NOT Gate (Inverter)
   - Concept: Connect both inputs of a single NAND gate together.
   - Logic: NOT (A AND A) = NOT A
   - NAND count: 1

2. AND Gate
   - Concept: Invert the output of a standard NAND gate using a second NAND as a NOT gate.
   - Logic: NOT (NOT (A AND B)) = A AND B
   - NAND count: 2

3. OR Gate
   - Concept: Invert each input using NAND NOT gates, then feed them into a third NAND gate.
   - Logic (De Morgan's Law): NOT (NOT A AND NOT B) = A OR B
   - NAND count: 3

4. XOR Gate
   - Concept: Constructed using 4 NAND gates:
     - Gate 1 = NOT (A AND B)
     - Gate 2 = NOT (A AND Gate 1)
     - Gate 3 = NOT (B AND Gate 1)
     - Output = NOT (Gate 2 AND Gate 3) = A XOR B
   - NAND count: 4
